How results are counted

Each signal is measured in R, where 1R is the distance between entry and stop loss.

  • Stop loss hit before TP1: −1R.
  • TP1 hit, half closed, rest stopped at entry: +{{ "%.2f"|format(params.tp1_r / 2) }}R.
  • TP1 and TP2 both hit: +{{ "%.2f"|format(params.tp1_r / 2 + params.tp2_r / 2) }}R.
  • Still open after the time limit: closed at the market price.

If one 15-minute candle touches both the stop and a target, we count the stop. Results exclude spread, commission and slippage, which vary by broker and reduce real returns.
